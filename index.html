      Supported CSV format, limits & honest limitations

      Supported input

      • Header-first CSV: first non-blank line is the header row; data rows follow.
      • Delimiters: comma, semicolon or tab (auto-detected or chosen manually).
      • Quoted fields with "" escaped quotes; embedded newlines in quotes; BOM tolerated; CRLF / LF / CR line endings.
      • Numbers: plain decimal or exponent notation with optional sign (-1.5, .5, 2.5e3). No unit suffixes, no thousands separators.
      • One configurable numeric elapsed-time column; channels are the finite numeric columns you select.

      Rules & limits

      • Max 5 MB and 50,000 data rows per import; larger input is rejected with a visible message.
      • Missing/blank/non-numeric samples become gaps: lines break, nothing is interpolated or zero-filled.
      • Rows whose time cell is not a finite number are skipped (with a warning) and break the trace.
      • Out-of-order timestamps are flagged; ticking “Sort samples by time” reorders by time.
      • Plots are bounded: at most 2,000 points per channel are drawn (min/max envelope — extrema are preserved).

      Honest limitations

      • Generic CSV only — it is not a universal VCDS multi-header parser and does not understand block headers, units rows or metadata sections.
      • No unit conversion; the scale field is a display-only multiplier.
      • Nothing is persisted: no localStorage, no uploads, no network — reloading the page clears everything.
